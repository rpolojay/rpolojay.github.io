How to Build US Credit as a Foreign Worker

📅 Updated June 2025 · ⏱ 7 min read · ⚓ For crew members & workers abroad

No US credit history means no credit cards, no car loans, no apartment leases in your name. But every crew member and foreign worker can build US credit from zero — even without a Social Security Number. Here's exactly how.

Why Credit Matters Even If You Don't Live in the US Permanently

You might think: "I work on a ship, I don't live in the US — why does this matter?" Here's why it matters more than you think:

  • Future visa applications — a US credit history demonstrates financial responsibility
  • Renting an apartment — if you ever transition to land-based work in the US
  • Better banking options — many premium accounts require a credit history
  • Lower insurance rates — in many US states, credit history affects insurance pricing
  • Building toward US residency — financial history can support future immigration processes

Step 1 — Get an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number)

1

Apply for an ITIN through the IRS

An ITIN is issued by the IRS to people who need to file US taxes but don't have a Social Security Number. As a foreign worker on a US-flagged cruise ship or with US income, you may be required to file taxes — and an ITIN lets you do that while also opening the door to credit-building products. Apply using IRS Form W-7. Processing takes 6–8 weeks.

Step 2 — Open a US Bank Account

2

Wise, Chime, or Charles Schwab

With your ITIN, open a US bank account. See our guide on opening a US bank account without a US address. Having an active US bank account with regular deposits (your salary) is a foundational step for credit applications.

Step 3 — Get a Secured Credit Card

3

The easiest credit-building tool that exists

A secured credit card requires a deposit — typically $200–$500 — which becomes your credit limit. You use it like a normal credit card, pay it off in full every month, and the issuer reports your payment history to the credit bureaus. After 6–12 months of perfect payments, many issuers refund your deposit and upgrade you to a regular card. Discover Secured and Capital One Secured both work with ITINs.

Step 4 — Become an Authorized User (Optional Boost)

4

If you have a trusted family member with US credit

If a family member with established US credit adds you as an authorized user on their credit card, their account history can appear on your credit report — instantly giving you months or years of positive history. This only works if their account has a long history of on-time payments and low balances. A bad account will hurt you, not help.

The 3 Rules That Build Credit Fast

  1. Pay in full, every month, on time. Never carry a balance. Interest charges don't build credit faster — they just cost you money.
  2. Keep utilization under 30%. If your limit is $500, never let your balance exceed $150 before the statement closes. Under 10% is even better.
  3. Never miss a payment. One missed payment can drop your score 50-100 points and stay on your report for 7 years. Set up automatic payments for at least the minimum.

What to Avoid

  • Don't apply for many cards at once. Each application creates a small temporary dip in your score. Space applications out.
  • Don't close your first card once you upgrade. Length of credit history matters — keep old accounts open even if unused.
  • Don't use credit repair companies promising fast fixes. Building real credit takes time. There's no legitimate shortcut.
💡 Start the ITIN Process Before Your Next Contract The ITIN application takes 6-8 weeks and requires some paperwork that's easier to handle on land with reliable internet and mail access. Start this process during your time at home — by the time your next contract begins, you'll be ready to open accounts and start building credit while you're earning. Building credit is a long game, but every month you wait to start is a month of progress lost.

Por qué el crédito importa aunque no vivas permanentemente en EE.UU.

  • Futuras solicitudes de visa — un historial de crédito en EE.UU. demuestra responsabilidad financiera
  • Alquilar un apartamento — si algún día transicionas a trabajo en tierra en EE.UU.

Paso 1 — Obtén un ITIN

1

Solicita un ITIN a través del IRS

Un ITIN es emitido por el IRS a personas que necesitan presentar impuestos en EE.UU. pero no tienen número de seguro social. Solicítalo con el Formulario W-7 del IRS. El procesamiento toma 6–8 semanas.

Paso 2 — Abre una cuenta bancaria en EE.UU.

2

Wise, Chime o Charles Schwab

Con tu ITIN, abre una cuenta bancaria en EE.UU. Ver nuestra guía sobre cómo abrir una cuenta bancaria sin dirección en EE.UU.

Paso 3 — Obtén una tarjeta de crédito garantizada

3

La herramienta más fácil para construir crédito

Una tarjeta garantizada requiere un depósito — típicamente $200–$500 — que se convierte en tu límite de crédito. La usas como una tarjeta normal, la pagas completamente cada mes, y el emisor reporta tu historial de pagos a las agencias de crédito.
