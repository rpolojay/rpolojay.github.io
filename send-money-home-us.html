Best Ways to Send Money Home from the US to Latin America

📅 Updated June 2025 · ⏱ 7 min read · ⚓ For crew members & workers abroad

Every year Latin American workers lose billions of dollars in unnecessary transfer fees. A crew member sending $500 home through Western Union can lose $25–$40 in fees and exchange rate markups. Wise or Remitly sends the same $500 for under $5. Here's exactly what to use.

The Real Cost of Sending Money Home

Most people focus on the transfer fee — the $5 or $10 shown on screen. But the bigger cost is hidden in the exchange rate. Services like Western Union and MoneyGram show you an exchange rate that's worse than the real mid-market rate. That gap — called the exchange rate markup — is where they make most of their money.

💸 Sending $500 home — what you actually pay

Western Union (cash pickup)$25–$40 in fees + markup
MoneyGram$15–$30 in fees + markup
Bank wire transfer$25–$50 flat fee
Remitly (economy)$3–$5 total
Wise$4–$7 total
Your savings per transfer$20–$35 every time

If you send $500 home every month, switching from Western Union to Wise or Remitly saves you $240–$420 per year. Over a 6-month contract, that's an extra $120–$210 that stays in your pocket.

⚠️ The Exchange Rate Trick

Always compare the total amount your family receives — not just the fee. A service advertising "$0 fee" may still give you a worse exchange rate than a service charging $4. Use Wise's rate comparison tool to see the true cost before every transfer.

How to Set Up Your First Transfer

  1. Download the Wise or Remitly app — both available on iOS and Android, free
  2. Create an account — you'll need your ID and US bank account or debit card
  3. Verify your identity — required by law, takes 1–2 days for first transfer
  4. Enter the amount and recipient details — your family's bank account or pickup location
  5. Compare the rate shown — always check what amount your family actually receives before confirming
  6. Send — save the transaction receipt

Tips for Crew Members Sending Money Home

  • Set up transfers before you board. Verify your account and do one test transfer while you still have easy internet access. Shipboard WiFi can make app verification difficult.
  • Send in larger amounts less frequently. Each transfer has a fixed fee component — sending $500 twice costs less in fees than sending $100 ten times.
  • Use the Wise multi-currency account. Hold your USD savings in Wise while at sea. When you're ready to send home, convert and transfer at the best available rate.
  • Have a backup method. Know two services in case one has an issue. Wise + Remitly is the best combination.
💡 The Crew Member Transfer Strategy Don't send money home every paycheck — the fixed fees add up. Instead, send one larger transfer per month. Save your contract earnings in a high-yield savings account, then transfer a planned monthly amount to your family. You save on fees AND earn interest on your savings between transfers.

Consejos para tripulantes que envían dinero a casa

  • Configura las transferencias antes de abordar. Verifica tu cuenta y haz una transferencia de prueba mientras aún tienes acceso fácil a internet. El WiFi del barco puede dificultar la verificación de apps.
  • Envía cantidades más grandes con menos frecuencia. Cada transferencia tiene un componente de tarifa fija — enviar $500 dos veces cuesta menos en comisiones que enviar $100 diez veces.
  • Usa la cuenta multimoneda de Wise. Guarda tus ahorros en USD en Wise mientras estás en el mar. Cuando estés listo para enviar a casa, convierte y transfiere a la mejor tasa disponible.
💡 La estrategia de transferencia del tripulante No envíes dinero a casa con cada pago — las tarifas fijas se acumulan. En cambio, envía una transferencia más grande por mes. Guarda tus ganancias del contrato en una cuenta de ahorro de alto rendimiento, luego transfiere un monto mensual planificado a tu familia.
