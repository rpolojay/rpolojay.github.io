How to Open a US Bank Account Without a US Address

📅 Updated June 2025 · ⏱ 7 min read · ⚓ For crew members & workers abroad

One of the biggest financial obstacles for cruise ship crew and Latin American workers in the US is banking — most US banks require a US address and Social Security Number. But there are real options that work even without those. Here's exactly what they are.

Why a US Bank Account Matters for Crew Members

Your cruise line pays your salary in USD. Without a US bank account, that money sits in a shipboard account or gets paid by check — both create problems when you want to save, invest, or transfer money efficiently.

A proper US bank account lets you:

  • Receive your salary directly via direct deposit
  • Transfer money home at the best rates using Wise
  • Keep your savings in a high-yield account earning 4–5%
  • Build a US financial footprint for future visa or credit applications
  • Avoid high check-cashing fees

Option 1 — Wise Multi-Currency Account (Best Starting Point)

⭐ Best for Crew Members
Wise Multi-Currency Account No US address needed
No SSN required US account number + routing number Hold USD, EUR, 50+ currencies Free to open

Wise gives you a real US account number and routing number — the same information a traditional bank gives you. Your employer can direct deposit your salary here. You can receive USD, hold it, and transfer it at excellent rates. No US address required — you can use your home country address.

What you need: Passport or national ID, home country address, email. No SSN, no US address required.

Option 2 — Chime (Online Bank)

Chime US address needed
No credit check No monthly fees Early direct deposit Debit card included

Chime is an online bank with no monthly fees and no minimum balance. If you have a US address — even a friend or family member's address — you can open a Chime account. Great for crew members who have some US connection.

What you need: SSN or ITIN, US address (can be a friend's), email. No credit check.

Option 3 — Charles Schwab International Account

Charles Schwab Bank Foreign address accepted
No foreign ATM fees worldwide Accepts foreign addresses No monthly fees Reimbursed ATM fees

Charles Schwab's checking account accepts foreign addresses and reimburses all ATM fees worldwide — making it excellent for crew members who need cash in various ports. Requires SSN or ITIN but accepts a foreign address.

What you need: SSN or ITIN, any address (can be foreign), passport.

Option 4 — ITIN + Traditional Bank

ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) Takes 6–8 weeks to get
Issued by IRS For non-US citizens Opens most US bank accounts No SSN needed

An ITIN is a tax ID number issued by the IRS to non-US citizens who need to file taxes or open bank accounts. With an ITIN, most traditional US banks — Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Chase — will open an account for you. Getting an ITIN takes 6–8 weeks and requires paperwork, but it opens the full US banking system.

What you need: Passport, visa or work authorization, IRS Form W-7, completed tax return.

The Recommended Setup for Crew Members

  1. Open a Wise account immediately — takes 10 minutes, no US address needed. Use this to receive your salary and hold your savings.
  2. Apply for an ITIN — start the paperwork before your first contract if possible. With an ITIN you can open full US bank accounts and eventually build US credit.
  3. Open a Charles Schwab account once you have an ITIN — the free worldwide ATM access is invaluable for crew members in various ports.
  4. Move savings to a high-yield account — once set up, move your contract savings into a high-yield savings account earning 4–5%.
