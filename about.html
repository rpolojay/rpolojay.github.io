About MoneyMadeSimple

Helping everyday people understand money — without the jargon, the complexity, or the judgment.

Why This Site Exists

Most personal finance content is written by people with finance degrees, for people who already understand finance. It's full of jargon, complicated strategies, and advice that assumes you're already comfortable with money.

MoneyMadeSimple was built for everyone else. For people who weren't taught about budgeting growing up. For people who feel overwhelmed every time they look at their bank account. For people who want to do better with money but don't know where to start.

Every article on this site is written with one goal: make it so simple and clear that anyone can understand it and take action today.

Por qué existe este sitio

La mayoría del contenido de finanzas personales está escrito por personas con títulos en finanzas, para personas que ya entienden de finanzas. Está lleno de tecnicismos, estrategias complicadas y consejos que asumen que ya te sientes cómodo con el dinero.

DineroFácil fue creado para todos los demás. Para personas que no aprendieron a presupuestar cuando eran jóvenes. Para personas que se sienten abrumadas cada vez que miran su cuenta bancaria. Para personas que quieren mejorar con el dinero pero no saben por dónde empezar.

Cada artículo en este sitio está escrito con un objetivo: hacerlo tan simple y claro que cualquiera pueda entenderlo y actuar hoy mismo.

What You'll Find Here

MoneyMadeSimple covers the four pillars of personal finance that matter most when you're starting out:

  • Budgeting — how to track and control your spending
  • Saving — building an emergency fund and saving for goals
  • Debt & Credit — understanding and improving your credit, paying off debt
  • Investing — growing your money over the long term

Every article is available in both English and Spanish — just use the EN / ES toggle in the top right corner of any page.
