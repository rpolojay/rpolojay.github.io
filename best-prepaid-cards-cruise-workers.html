Best Prepaid Debit Cards for Cruise Ship Workers

📅 Updated June 2025 · ⏱ 6 min read · ⚓ For crew members & workers abroad

When you're in 10 different countries in a month, your debit card matters more than you think. Foreign transaction fees and ATM charges can quietly eat $50-100 of your savings per contract. Here's how to pick a card that doesn't punish you for working at sea.

Why Your Card Choice Matters at Sea

A typical bank debit card charges 1-3% on every foreign transaction, plus $2-5 per international ATM withdrawal. If you withdraw cash in 8 different ports during a contract — common for crew members — those fees alone can cost $40-80. Over a year of contracts, that's $100-200 disappearing in fees you never see itemized clearly.

The right prepaid or debit card eliminates almost all of this.

Best Cards for Crew Members

⭐ Best Overall
Wise Debit Card No foreign fees
Multi-currency Mid-market exchange rate 2 free ATM withdrawals/month No US address needed

The Wise card automatically converts your balance to local currency at the real exchange rate — no markup. Hold USD, EUR, and 40+ currencies in one account. First 2 ATM withdrawals per month (up to $100 total) are free; small fee after that. This pairs perfectly with the Wise account we recommend for receiving your salary.

✅ Best for: Multi-country contracts, holding multiple currencies, overall value

⭐ Best for ATM Access
Charles Schwab Debit Card All ATM fees reimbursed
No foreign transaction fees Unlimited ATM reimbursement Worldwide Requires ITIN/SSN

Schwab reimburses ALL ATM fees worldwide — even fees charged by the ATM operator itself. No foreign transaction fees on purchases either. This is the gold standard for international cash access, but requires a Schwab checking account which needs an ITIN or SSN.

✅ Best for: Frequent ATM withdrawals in multiple countries

Revolut Free tier limited
Multi-currency Crypto features Free ATM up to limit

Similar to Wise with multi-currency support. Free plan includes limited free ATM withdrawals per month, then a small percentage fee. Popular in Europe — good option if your contract focuses on European itineraries.

✅ Best for: European-focused contracts, additional currency flexibility

Traditional Bank Debit Card 3% + $5 per withdrawal
Widely accepted High fees abroad

Most traditional bank cards (from banks back home) charge significant foreign transaction fees and ATM fees. Fine as a backup card, but shouldn't be your primary card at sea — the fees compound quickly across a multi-port contract.

⚠️ Use as: Backup only — not your primary card

The Recommended Setup for Crew Members

  1. Start with Wise — no address or SSN required, get your card before your first contract
  2. Add Charles Schwab once you have an ITIN — use it as your primary ATM card for unlimited reimbursed withdrawals
  3. Keep one traditional card as backup — for situations where Wise or Schwab aren't accepted
  4. Load your card before port days — transfer only what you plan to spend, keep the rest in savings earning interest
💡 The Card Strategy That Saves the Most Don't keep your full balance loaded on a spending card. Keep your savings in a high-yield savings account earning 4-5%, and transfer only your planned spending amount to your Wise or Schwab card before each port day. This protects your savings from impulse spending AND earns interest on money that would otherwise sit idle on a card.
