What to Do Next

Now that you have your numbers, here's how to put them into action:

  1. Open a separate savings account and set up an automatic transfer for your savings amount on payday.
  2. Track your needs for one month — use bank statements to check you're within the target.
  3. Set a wants limit — use cash or a prepaid card to make it tangible.
  4. Review monthly and recalculate whenever your income changes.
