Best Free Budgeting Apps in 2025

📅 Updated June 2025 · ⏱ 7 min read · 👤 Beginner-friendly

The right budgeting app can transform your finances — or sit unused on your phone. The best app isn't the one with the most features. It's the one you'll actually use. Here are the top free options and how to pick the right one for you.

What Makes a Great Budgeting App?

Before diving in, here's what actually matters in a budgeting app:

  • Easy to set up — if it takes an hour to configure, you won't stick with it
  • Syncs with your bank — manual entry is the fastest way to abandon an app
  • Visual and clear — you should see your spending picture at a glance
  • Actually free — not "free" with a premium upsell on every screen

The Best Free Budgeting Apps

📊

Mint

Free · iOS & Android
100% Free
★★★★☆ 4.2/5

The most popular budgeting app in the US. Connects to your bank, credit cards, and investments to give you a complete financial picture automatically. Categorises transactions and shows where your money goes.

  • Completely free
  • Bank sync works well
  • Credit score tracking
  • Bill reminders
  • Ads in the app
  • Can be glitchy
  • Less control than YNAB
✅ Best for: Beginners who want automatic tracking with zero setup effort
💚

YNAB (You Need A Budget)

Free trial · iOS & Android
Free 34-day trial
★★★★★ 4.8/5

The gold standard of budgeting apps. Uses the zero-based budgeting method — you assign every dollar a job before the month starts. Steeper learning curve but dramatically more effective for people serious about changing their finances.

  • Most effective method
  • Excellent education
  • Bank sync
  • Great community
  • $14.99/month after trial
  • Learning curve
  • Requires active use
✅ Best for: Anyone serious about zero-based budgeting and willing to pay after trial

EveryDollar

Free version · iOS & Android
Free (basic)
★★★★☆ 4.0/5

Created by Dave Ramsey, EveryDollar uses zero-based budgeting in a simpler, cleaner interface than YNAB. The free version requires manual transaction entry — bank sync requires a paid upgrade.

  • Simple interface
  • Zero-based budgeting
  • Free version available
  • No bank sync for free
  • Basic free features
  • Less robust than YNAB
✅ Best for: Dave Ramsey followers who want a simpler zero-based budgeting app
📱

Goodbudget

Free version · iOS & Android
Free (limited)
★★★★☆ 4.1/5

A digital version of the envelope method. You manually allocate money to virtual envelopes and track spending against each one. No bank sync — all manual entry. Works great for couples budgeting together.

  • Envelope method digital
  • Great for couples
  • Simple concept
  • Cross-device sync
  • Manual entry only
  • 10 envelope limit (free)
  • No investment tracking
✅ Best for: People who like the envelope method but don't want to use physical cash

💡 The Best App Is the One You Use Don't spend weeks researching apps. Pick one, use it for 30 days, and see if it fits. The perfect app used imperfectly beats the perfect app left uninstalled. Start with Mint if you want zero friction, or YNAB's free trial if you want maximum effectiveness.

¿Qué hace que una app de presupuesto sea excelente?

Antes de comenzar, esto es lo que realmente importa en una app de presupuesto:

  • Fácil de configurar — si tarda una hora en configurarse, no la seguirás usando
  • Se sincroniza con tu banco — la entrada manual es la forma más rápida de abandonar una app
  • Visual y clara — deberías ver tu panorama de gastos de un vistazo
  • Realmente gratis — no "gratis" con una oferta premium en cada pantalla

Las mejores apps gratuitas de presupuesto

Mint

Gratis · iOS y Android
100% Gratis
★★★★☆ 4.2/5

La app de presupuesto más popular. Se conecta a tu banco, tarjetas de crédito e inversiones para darte una imagen financiera completa automáticamente. Categoriza transacciones y muestra a dónde va tu dinero.
